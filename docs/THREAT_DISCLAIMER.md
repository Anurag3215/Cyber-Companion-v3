# Threat Intelligence & Heuristics Disclaimer

**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026  
**Application:** Cyber Companion v3

---

## 1. Automated Analysis Notice
The evaluations, classifications, and security scores generated by Cyber Companion v3 are produced through automated deterministic heuristics and reputation feeds. While our multi-engine system maintains high accuracy (statistically verified at $>99\%$ precision), no automated defense system can guarantee detection of 100% of emerging zero-day cyber threats.

---

## 2. False Positives & False Negatives
- **Newly Registered Domains (NRDs)**: Brand-new phishing websites launched within minutes of an attack may not yet appear in public threat feeds. Always exercise personal caution when prompted to enter credentials.
- **Benign Suspicious Patterns**: Legitimate developmental tools, internal network resources, or experimental top-level domains may occasionally exhibit traits shared with malicious campaigns.
- **Reporting Inaccuracies**: Users who encounter suspected false positives or undetected threats are encouraged to report samples to our issue tracker: `https://github.com/Anurag3215/Cyber-Companion-v3/issues`.

---

## 3. Advisory Nature of Recommendations
The security tips, permission auditing guidance, and scoring recommendations provided in this application are for general consumer educational and defensive awareness purposes. They do not constitute formal legal, corporate compliance, or incident response counsel.
