### Full Description (Max 4000 chars)
Protect your mobile digital life with **Cyber Companion v3**, the open-source, privacy-first cybersecurity guardian.

Unlike conventional security utilities that track your online footprint, Cyber Companion is architected from the ground up on **Zero-Trust & Zero-PII principles**. All permission audits occur strictly on your device. Your Wi-Fi credentials and scanned browsing links are never sold, profiled, or tied to your identity.

#### 🛡️ KEY FEATURES:
- **Instant QR & Link Safety Scanner**:
  Scan QR codes from restaurant tables, posters, or messages without fear of quishing or phishing attacks. Our multi-engine threat analyzer checks lookalike homoglyphs, punycode spoofing, and malicious redirect chains in real time.
- **Wi-Fi Telemetry & Rogue AP Guard**:
  Instantly diagnose your wireless connection. Flags unencrypted open networks, legacy WEP/WPA vulnerabilities, and captive portal risks before you enter sensitive credentials.
- **On-Device Permission Auditor**:
  Identify apps requesting sensitive permissions (Background Location, Camera, Microphone, SMS). View risk severity and actionable steps to reclaim your privacy.
- **Deterministic 0–100 Security Score**:
  No confusing jargon or AI hallucinations. Receive a mathematically verified security score powered by transparent, deterministic formulas across Network, Permissions, Threats, and Awareness.
- **Bite-Sized Daily Cyber Awareness**:
  Learn practical digital self-defense with 60-word micro-tips written for everyday users. Track your awareness streaks ethically—with pause toggles and zero guilt penalties.

#### 🔒 PRIVACY BY DESIGN:
- Zero account required.
- Zero tracking ads or third-party behavioral analytics.
- On-device package evaluation.
- Open-source and verifiable codebase.

## 3. Required App Permissions & Justifications
- **Camera (`CAMERA`)**: Required solely for scanning physical QR codes via live camera preview. No images are saved or transmitted to cloud servers.
- **Wi-Fi State (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`)**: Used to inspect encryption standards (WPA3/WPA2/WEP) of the current active Wi-Fi connection.
